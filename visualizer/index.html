2. HashMap buckets — where does a key land?

Bucket = (hashCode & 0x7fffffff) % 16. Type a key and see its bucket. Keys landing in the same bucket chain together — that is a collision.

3. Top-K Frequent — why a min-heap?

Frequencies: 1→3, 2→2, 3→1, K = 2. The heap keeps only the 2 biggest — the smallest is dropped as soon as the heap grows past K. Sorting everything would be O(n log n); the heap is O(n log K) and O(K) memory.
